How a tier is decided

  1. Raw vitals go through one saved pipeline: median imputation, scaling and one-hot encoding, then the {{ card.model_name }}. SMOTE balances classes during training only.
  2. Probabilities are calibrated with Platt scaling on non-oversampled folds, so 30% means roughly 30%.
  3. If P(High) is at least {{ card.high_threshold }} the patient is High. The threshold was picked on out-of-fold training predictions to reach {{ card.target_high_recall|pct }} High recall.
  4. Clinical safety rules (low SpO₂, hypotension, GCS 8 or less and so on) can raise the tier but never lower it.
  5. A nurse or doctor can override the result. Every override and its reason is stored.
